My Personality and Interests

My personality, interests, and hobbies play an important role in how I approach my academic life and personal development.

My Strongest Traits

Hardworking

I consider myself a hardworking person. I try to remain committed to my responsibilities and continue working toward my goals even when something becomes challenging.

Problem Solving

I enjoy solving problems, especially when working with technology. Problem solving helps me approach difficult academic tasks by breaking them down into smaller and more manageable parts.

Critical Thinking

Critical thinking is important to me because it helps me examine problems carefully before deciding how to approach them. This is particularly useful when studying computing and programming.

My Hobbies and Interests

Volleyball

Volleyball is one of my interests. It provides an opportunity to stay active and take a break from academic responsibilities.

Music

Music is also an important part of my life. I often listen to music while studying because it helps me focus. It can also help me when I am stressed or experiencing brain fog while studying.

My Hero

My mother is someone I admire and look up to.

She has played an important role in my life by providing support, encouragement, and guidance. Her influence has taught me the importance of working hard, staying determined, and continuing to pursue my goals even when things become difficult.

I consider her a role model because of the positive influence she has had on my personal development and the motivation she gives me to continue working toward my future goals.

"Success is the result of hard work, persistence, and continuing to move forward."

Artificial Intelligence and My Education

The statement that LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot must be used as a tool and not a crutch means that students should use artificial intelligence to support their learning rather than allowing it to replace their own thinking and effort.

As a student, I believe AI can be useful when it helps me understand difficult concepts, explore different explanations, and identify areas where I need to improve. However, I should still understand the work myself and be responsible for what I submit.

I use AI to study and to help rephrase my work so that I can communicate my ideas more clearly. I believe AI should be used to assist learning rather than simply completing academic work without the student understanding it.
